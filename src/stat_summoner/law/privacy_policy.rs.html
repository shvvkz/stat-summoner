1//! # Privacy Policy for Stat Summoner Bot (Version 0.5.1)
2//!
3//! **Last updated: 10/03/2024**
4//!
5//! ---
6//!
7//! ## 1. Introduction
8//!
9//! This Privacy Policy describes how the **Stat Summoner Bot** ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects the information provided by users ("you") when using the Bot on Discord. By using the Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
10//!
11//! ## 2. Data Collection and Use
12//!
13//! ### 2.1 Information Collected
14//!
15//! The Bot collects minimal data necessary to provide its functionalities, including:
16//!
17//! - **Discord User IDs**: To identify users interacting with the Bot.
18//! - **Discord Channel IDs**: To send messages to the appropriate channels.
19//! - **Summoner Names and Tags**: Provided voluntarily by users to retrieve statistics from the Riot Games API.
20//!
21//! ### 2.2 Use of Collected Information
22//!
23//! The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:
24//!
25//! - **Providing Services**: To execute commands and provide League of Legends statistics and information as requested by users.
26//! - **Improving Functionality**: To enhance the user experience and improve the Bot's features.
27//!
28//! ## 3. Data Storage and Security
29//!
30//! - **Data Storage**: The collected data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. Only the necessary data is retained, and no unnecessary personal information is stored.
31//! - **Data Protection**: Measures are in place to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
32//! - **Data Retention**: Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.
33//!
34//! ## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
35//!
36//! - **Third-Party Services**: The Bot interacts with the Riot Games API to retrieve game data. Summoner Names and Tags are shared with the API solely to fetch the requested information.
37//! - **No Selling of Data**: The Bot does not sell, trade, or rent users' personal identification information to others.
38//! - **Legal Requirements**: The Bot may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
39//!
40//! ## 5. Children's Privacy
41//!
42//! The Bot is intended for use by individuals who are at least 16 years old. If you are under 16, please do not use the Bot or provide any personal information.
43//!
44//! ## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
45//!
46//! The Bot's developer reserves the right to update or change this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy. Users are encouraged to periodically review this policy for any changes.
47//!
48//! ## 7. Your Acceptance of These Terms
49//!
50//! By using the Bot, you signify your acceptance of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to this policy, please do not use the Bot. Continued use of the Bot following the posting of changes to this policy will be deemed your acceptance of those changes.
